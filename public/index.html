About Me

Hi. I’m Giulia, nice to meet you.

“The brain is imagination, and that was exciting to me; I wanted to build a chip that could imagine something.” — Misha Mahowald

I hold a Bachelor’s in Biomedical Engineering and a Master’s in Neuroengineering (with honors) from the University of Genoa, with a thesis on neuromorphic vision at King’s College London.

After a year as a software engineer at the Italian Institute of Technology, I earned my PhD from The University of Manchester, receiving the President’s Doctoral Scholar Award. My research focused on bio-inspired visual attention for the iCub humanoid robot using neuromorphic hardware.

Currently, I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at the Czech Technical University in Prague, leading the ENDEAVOR project on event-driven active vision.
