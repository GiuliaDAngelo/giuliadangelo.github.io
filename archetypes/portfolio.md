**Instructions for Content:**

This area is for the main body of your case study.

*   **Expand on the solution:** Provide more detail than the front matter `solution` field.
*   **Showcase your process:** Include details about research, design mockups, development stages, and outcomes.
*   **Use visuals:** Embed images, diagrams, or videos to illustrate your work.
    *   Example: `![Mockup showing the dashboard](images/portfolio/your-project/dashboard.png)`
*   **Quantify results:** If possible, use metrics to demonstrate the project's success (e.g., "improved user engagement by 20%," "reduced processing time by 50%").
