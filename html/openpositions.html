The Neuro-inspired Perception and Cognition (NPC) Lab at Czech Technical University in Prague focuses on neuro-inspired approaches to perception and cognition. Our research spans neuromorphic sensing and computing, active vision, embodied AI, and cognitive robotics.

Spontaneous Applications

Interested in joining the NPC lab?

We are always looking for talented and motivated students and researchers. If you would like to work with us, please feel free to send us a spontaneous application.

We welcome applications for:

  • PhD positions
  • Postdoctoral researchers For collaboration on grant proposals: GACR Postdoctoral Incoming fellowships and Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions for Postdoctoral Fellowships (in the Czech Republic MSCA proposals reaching 70\% of the score are funded, which is a significant advantage)
  • Master's/Bachelor's thesis projects
  • Research internships Typically 6-9 months
  • Visiting researchers

How to apply:

Please send your application to giulia.dangelo@fel.cvut.cz with "Spontaneous Application - [Position Type]" in the subject line.

Include:

  • CV (including publications if applicable)
  • Academic transcripts (for student positions)
  • Optional: Motivation letter (no more tha 1 page)

Note: Due to the high volume of applications, we may not be able to respond to all spontaneous applications immediately. However, we will make sure to contact you if there is a good match with our current or upcoming projects.
