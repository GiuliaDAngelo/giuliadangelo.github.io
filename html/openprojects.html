Spiking motion detector: iCub playing pinball

Bachelor/Master Project

Supervision: Dr. Giulia D'Angelo (CTU), Mazdak Fatahi (University of Lille)

Context: Spiking Neural Network (SNN) for rapid flipper control

Objectives: iCub will play pinball by detecting the ball's movements and pressing the flippers with its hands to win.

Workplan: 1. Master (SNN implemenetation of the motion detector and deployment on iCub); Bachelor (iCub event-driven setup and inverse kinematics movements to press the flippers) 2. Model development on the robot 3. Testing on the robot

References: SNN motion detector: D'Angelo, Giulia, et al. "Event-based eccentric motion detection exploiting time difference encoding." Frontiers in neuroscience 14 (2020): 451.

Spiking Neural Network for visual exploration: Object learning

Bachelor/Master Project

Supervision: Dr. Giulia D'Angelo (CTU)

Context: Spiking Neural Network (SNN) for alternative object discrimination.

Objectives: iCub will hold an object in its hands and visually explore it while moving its wrist to learn the object through sensorimotor contingiencies.

Workplan: 1. Master (SNN implementation leveraging contrastive learning through time (CLTT)); Bachelor (iCub event-driven setup with neck and wrist movement capabilities) 2. Model development on the robot 3. Testing on the robot

References: CLTT: Aubret, Arthur, et al. "Learning object semantic similarity with self-supervision." 2024 IEEE International Conference on Development and Learning (ICDL). IEEE, 2024.
